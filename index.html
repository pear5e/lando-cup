The Format

A Ryder Cup–style battle: two teams of six, captains draft the squads at the welcome dinner, and every match across the weekend is worth 1 point.

How it works

Twelve golfers split into two teams of six. Two captains (selected later) draft their squads Thursday night after the practice round.

Friday and Saturday feature two-man team matches — morning and afternoon. Sunday is individual net match play, everyone out on the course with a point on the line. Most points wins the Cup.

All scoring is net, so handicaps keep every match honest.
